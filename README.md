# Aura Ventures Hub

Build an ultra-premium, production-grade corporate website for "Aura Dynamics Private Limited" — a high-scale digital aggregator and multi-service technology holding corporation based in India.

CRITICAL DESIGN MANDATE (HUMAN-DESIGNED, ZERO AI CLICHÉS):
- Avoid generic AI design tropes: NO neon purple/cyan gradient blobs, NO generic centered 3-card grids on every section, NO floating 3D glass balls, and NO repetitive card patterns.
- Adopt a bespoke editorial and institutional design language inspired by Stripe Press, DeepMind, Linear, and Alphabet:
  * Asymmetric layouts, split-screen sections, and sticky left-hand section headers with scrolling right-hand content.
  * Deep Midnight Navy (#1E2D3D) as the primary anchor, complemented by Warm Champagne Gold (#B79761), crisp off-white canvas (#F8FAFC), and sharp 1px hairline borders (border-slate-200/80 or border-[#1E2D3D]/10).
  * High-density typography: Clean geometric sans (Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans) with tight negative tracking (-0.025em) on display headlines, generous line-height on long-form body text, and small uppercase tracked kicker labels (`text-[11px] font-semibold tracking-widest text-[#B79761] uppercase`).
  * Real corporate touches: Corporate Identification Number (CIN) placeholder, registered office details in Patna, Bihar, regulatory compliance badges, and clean breadcrumbs.
